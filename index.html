Data notes. Current operating state is shown as of — and is separate from annual capacity or production data. Published monthly records are shown inside each facility profile when available. “Operational” includes facilities listed as operating, restarting/restarted, ramping up, expanding, or included within an operating parent site. “Not operating yet” covers planned and under-construction projects. “Offline / shut down” covers shut-down, idle, and no-active-capacity facilities. Detailed status from the source sheets remains visible. Production and capacity are in thousand metric tonnes per year; later-year values may be estimates or forecasts.
